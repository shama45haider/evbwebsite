Before You Visit

Before You Come In

A quick checklist so you know what to bring on your visit.

  1. Watch, bracelet, extra links, box, papers, warranty card, and any service records. Full sets get stronger offers — but watches without box or papers still qualify.

  2. New York State law — every seller needs a government-issued photo ID. Driver's license, passport, or state ID. No exceptions.

  3. Every watch is inspected at the counter in front of you — movement, dial, case, serial numbers, and overall condition. We explain what we see before we quote.

  4. Not sure if your watch qualifies or what it's worth? Text clear photos of the dial, case back, bracelet, and papers to 917-608-8939. We'll tell you straight before you make the trip.

Swiss Luxury

Buy & Sell Rolex in NYC

Rolex is the most traded luxury watch in the world — and the brand we see most at our counter. We buy and sell Submariner, Datejust, GMT-Master II, Daytona, Explorer, Yacht-Master, and more. Walk in with yours and we'll make you an offer on the spot.

Models We Buy & Sell

  • Submariner & Submariner Date — all references
  • Datejust & Datejust 41 — all dial and bezel combos
  • GMT-Master II — Pepsi, Batman, Coke, Root Beer
  • Daytona — steel, two-tone, and precious metal
  • Explorer & Explorer II
  • Yacht-Master & Yacht-Master II
  • Day-Date, Sky-Dweller, Milgauss, Air-King

What Affects Your Price

Rolex pricing comes down to reference, condition, and completeness. Here's what we evaluate:

  • Reference number and production era
  • Dial condition — original dial vs service dial matters
  • Bracelet stretch, end links, and clasp condition
  • Box, papers, warranty card, and tags
  • Service history and recent maintenance
  • Market demand for that specific reference

Swiss Heritage

Buy & Sell Omega in NYC

Omega has one of the strongest resale markets outside of Rolex. We buy and sell Speedmaster, Seamaster, Constellation, and De Ville — from modern references to well-kept vintage pieces.

Models We Buy & Sell

  • Speedmaster — Moonwatch, Reduced, Racing, '57
  • Seamaster — Diver 300M, Planet Ocean, Aqua Terra
  • Constellation — modern and vintage
  • De Ville — Prestige and Tourbillon lines
  • Vintage Omega — chronographs, dress watches, divers

Speedmaster & Seamaster Notes

The Speedmaster Professional and Seamaster Diver 300M are two of the most common Omegas we see. For Speedmasters, we check the movement (caliber 1861 vs 3861), bezel insert condition, and whether it's a full set. For Seamasters, ceramic vs aluminum bezel, bracelet vs rubber, and service history all factor in. Bring everything you have.

French Luxury

Buy & Sell Cartier in NYC

Cartier sits at the intersection of jewelry and horology — and the resale market reflects that. We buy and sell Santos, Tank, Ballon Bleu, Panthère, and other Cartier watch lines in steel, two-tone, and precious metal.

Models We Buy & Sell

  • Santos — Medium, Large, de Santos
  • Tank — Must, Française, Louis Cartier, Solo
  • Ballon Bleu — all sizes
  • Panthère — modern and vintage
  • Ronde, Drive, Pasha

What We Look For

Cartier watches are evaluated on case and bracelet condition, crystal clarity, movement function, and originality of dial and hands. Precious metal Cartier pieces — gold Santos, gold Tank — command strong offers. Box and papers add value, especially on newer models. Text us photos of the case back and clasp code if you're unsure of the exact reference.

Rolex Sister Brand

Buy & Sell Tudor in NYC

Tudor has exploded in the secondary market — Black Bay, Pelagos, and Ranger references hold value well and move fast. We buy and sell Tudor watches for customers who want Rolex-level build quality at a different price point.

Models We Buy & Sell

  • Black Bay — 41, 58, GMT, Chrono, Pro
  • Pelagos — standard and FXD
  • Ranger — current and heritage-inspired
  • Royal & Glamour — dress and everyday
  • Vintage Tudor — Submariner, Prince Date, chronographs

Black Bay Pricing Factors

Black Bay 58 and GMT references are the most actively traded Tudors right now. We look at bezel insert condition, bracelet vs fabric strap, full-set completeness, and whether it's an ETA or in-house movement generation. Vintage Tudor Submariners and "Snowflake" dials are evaluated separately — bring them in and we'll assess authenticity and condition in person.

Patek Philippe transactions require thorough authentication — dial, case, movement, extract or certificate of origin, and provenance where available. Models We Buy & Sell

  • Royal Oak Concept — by evaluation

Royal Oak Evaluation

We inspect tapisserie dial condition, bracelet screw tightness and stretch, case sharpness, and movement service history. Full sets with AP warranty card and box command premium pricing. For Offshore and vintage references, we evaluate each piece individually — text photos of the dial, case back, and clasp to 917-608-8939 for a preliminary assessment.

Collectors & Enthusiasts

Buy & Sell Vintage Sport Watches in NYC

Beyond the big Swiss houses, we buy and sell vintage and neo-vintage sport watches from brands that collectors actively hunt — Heuer, Breitling, Zenith, TAG Heuer, and classic dive chronographs from the 1960s through the 2000s.

Brands & Styles We Buy & Sell

  • Heuer & TAG Heuer — Carrera, Monaco, Autavia, Aquaracer
  • Breitling — Navitimer, Superocean, Chronomat
  • Zenith — El Primero chronographs
  • Universal Genève — Polerouter, Compax
  • Vintage divers — 60s–90s sport references across brands

Vintage Watch Considerations

Vintage pieces are evaluated on originality — dial, hands, bezel, crown, and movement should match the era. Repainted dials, aftermarket parts, and incorrect bezels affect value significantly. We prefer running movements but will evaluate non-running vintage watches on a case-by-case basis. Service history and provenance help. Bring the watch as-is — don't polish it before coming in.

Neo-Vintage & Limited Editions

Watches from the 1990s and 2000s — sometimes called neo-vintage — are having a moment. Limited editions, discontinued references, and "halo" models from major brands often qualify even if they're not from the traditional vintage era. Text us the reference and era and we'll tell you if it's in our wheelhouse.

What to Bring

  • The watch on its current bracelet or strap
  • Extra links, original strap, or deployant clasp
  • Box, papers, and service receipts if you have them
  • Any documentation on provenance or prior ownership
  • Valid photo ID — required by New York State law

Beyond the Mainstream

Niche & Collector Watch Brands

Not every valuable watch says Rolex on the dial. We buy and sell haute horology and collector-favorite brands that trade actively on the secondary market — always authenticated in store, always priced to current comps.

IWC

Pilot's Watch, Big Pilot, Portugieser, Aquatimer, and Ingenieur references. We evaluate case condition, service history, and whether it's a full set — IWC papers and box add real value on modern pieces.

Jaeger-LeCoultre

Reverso, Master Control, Polaris, and Duometre lines. JLC is pure movement house credibility — we inspect dial originality, case wear, and bracelet or strap completeness before quoting.

Panerai

Luminor, Radiomir, Submersible, and Luminor Due. Panerai pricing hinges on reference, limited runs, and condition of the case and crown guard assembly. Bring extra straps if you have them.

Grand Seiko

Spring Drive, Hi-Beat, GMT, and heritage dress references. Grand Seiko has a dedicated collector base — we check dial finishing, case sharpness, and whether box and papers are included.

Vacheron Constantin

Overseas, Patrimony, Fiftysix, and Historiques. High-value VC pieces get the same thorough authentication as Patek and AP — text reference photos before visiting on complicated or vintage examples.

Blancpain

Fifty Fathoms, Bathyscaphe, and Villeret dress watches. Blancpain divers are niche but highly respected — bezel insert condition, movement service history, and completeness all factor into the offer.
